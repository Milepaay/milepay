Process

Three steps to protected work

No lawyers. No disputes over "did you deliver?" The contract is the code.

01
Client deposits funds

Client creates an escrow, defines milestones, and deposits the full amount in USDC. Funds are locked in a Soroban smart contract on Stellar — untouchable until milestones are approved.

02
Freelancer delivers

Work is done milestone by milestone. The freelancer delivers each piece, knowing payment is already secured — no risk of non-payment, no awkward invoice follow-ups.

03
Payment releases instantly

Client approves the milestone — funds transfer to the freelancer's Stellar wallet in under 5 seconds. No bank, no wire transfer, no waiting 30 days.

Features

Built for real freelance work

On-chain escrow

Funds sit in a Soroban smart contract — not a company's bank account. Immutable, auditable, trustless. No one can run with the money.

Milestone-based release

Break any project into milestones with individual amounts. Client approves each one independently — no bulk transfers, no disputes over partial work.

On-chain dispute resolution

If things go sideways, either party can raise a dispute. A neutral arbitrator resolves it on-chain — no chargebacks, no power imbalance, no ghosting.

Near-zero fees

Stellar transactions cost fractions of a cent. A $5,000 escrow doesn't lose $150 to the network. Built for real money, real freelancers, real margins.
